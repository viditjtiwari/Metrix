The proposed project, \textbf{METRIX}, is an enterprise-grade web-based Online Verification and Digital Certification System designed to digitize the statutory regulatory lifecycle of commercial weighing and measuring instruments under the Legal Metrology Act, 2009. The system automates instrument registration, application submission, dynamic scheduling, standardized field inspection observation recording, tamper-evident digital certificate issuance, and compliance tracking. 

Stakeholders can register instruments and submit verification applications online, while authorized Legal Metrology Officers (LMOs) and Government Approved Test Centres (GATCs) can schedule inspections, validate measurements against Maximum Permissible Error (MPE) thresholds, and issue cryptographically secured digital certificates embedded with verifiable Quick Response (QR) codes. Consumers and enforcement authorities can independently authenticate certificates in real time via a public verification portal. The platform incorporates role-based access control (RBAC), automated expiry notifications, and compliance analytics, providing a unified regulatory solution.
\end{tcolorbox}

%======================================================================
% 1. ORIGIN OF THE PROPOSAL (MOTIVATION)
%======================================================================
\section{Origin of the Proposal (Motivation)}
The verification of weighing and measuring instruments is legally mandated to protect consumer rights and guarantee trade equity. In the current operational landscape, verification involves multi-party coordination across instrument owners, field officers, and testing laboratories. However, existing workflows rely overwhelmingly on physical ledgers, manual challan handling, paper certificates, and physical brass/lead stamping. 

The \textbf{SIH26036} problem statement identifies the imperative to eliminate paper-based bottlenecks and establish an authoritative, centralized digital record. The core motivators include:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Eliminating Paperwork:} Transitioning from manual challans and physical registers to automated web filings.
    \item \textbf{Centralizing Metrology Records:} Establishing a single source of truth for instrument lifecycles across jurisdictions.
    \item \textbf{Real-Time Application Tracking:} Providing live status tracking from submission to certificate issuance.
    \item \textbf{Optimized Scheduling:} Dynamic allocation of field inspections to LMOs and specialized GATC laboratories.
    \item \textbf{Standardized Metrological Records:} Capturing digital observations (load repeatability, eccentricity, error calculations) on-site.
    \item \textbf{Anti-Counterfeit Certificates:} Generating cryptographically signed PDF certificates with dynamic QR verification.
    \item \textbf{Proactive Expiry Enforcement:} Automated tracking of validity intervals and scheduled re-verification notifications.
\end{itemize}

%======================================================================
% 2. DEFINITION OF THE PROBLEM
%======================================================================
\section{Definition of the Problem}
Verification and re-verification of commercial measuring instruments encompass a distributed sequence of statutory checks executed by heterogeneous actors. Manual or fragmented record-keeping makes it arduous to monitor application queues, allocate inspection duties, preserve complete instrument histories, prevent certificate forgery, and enforce timely re-verification.

\textbf{The METRIX Solution:} Developing a unified, accessible, and secure web-based verification and digital certification platform that coordinates the complete instrument lifecycle—from registration and application submission to scheduling, on-site testing, digital certificate generation, and automated re-verification tracking. The platform provides isolated, role-tailored workspaces for Commercial Establishments, Legal Metrology Officers (LMOs), Government Approved Test Centres (GATCs), and System Administrators.

% 3. OBJECTIVES
%======================================================================
\section{Project Objectives}
The project delivers fourteen targeted objectives to establish an automated legal metrology framework:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Secure Web Platform:} Engineer a responsive, role-partitioned web application with robust authentication.
    \item \textbf{Stakeholder Profile Management:} Provide self-service digital onboarding for commercial businesses and repairers.
    \item \textbf{Centralized Instrument Registry:} Catalog instruments with specifications, serial numbers, accuracy classes, and capacities.
    \item \textbf{Online Application Lifecycle:} Support digital submission for initial verification and periodic re-verification with fee challans.
    \item \textbf{Transparent Status Tracking:} Provide multi-state tracking (\textit{Draft, Submitted, Under Review, Scheduled, Verified, Certified}).
    \item \textbf{Intelligent Scheduling \& Allocation:} Allocate inspection workloads to designated LMOs and accredited GATCs.
    \item \textbf{Digital Metrological Logging:} Standardize on-site checklists for tare error, repeatability, and MPE tolerance limits.
    \item \textbf{Tamper-Evident Certification:} Generate official PDF verification certificates using server-rendered vector layouts.
    \item \textbf{QR-Based Anti-Counterfeiting:} Embed cryptographic SHA-256 hashes and verification URLs into high-density QR codes.
    \item \textbf{Zero-Login Public Verification:} Enable citizens and enforcement squads to verify instrument calibration by scanning QR codes.
    \item \textbf{Complete Regulatory Audit Trails:} Maintain immutable transaction logs of all inspection decisions and certificate issuances.
    \item \textbf{Validity \& Expiry Monitoring:} Automatically monitor certificate lifecycles and calculate impending expirations.
    \item \textbf{Automated Compliance Reminders:} Dispatch automated re-verification alerts to prevent statutory non-compliance.
    \item \textbf{Role-Tailored Dashboards:} Deliver real-time analytics on clearance rates, inspection pendency, and district compliance.
\end{enumerate}

% 4. WORK PLAN
%======================================================================
\section{Work Plan}
The project is planned as a software-only web platform executed over an intensive four-week delivery timeline structured into four progressive phases:

\begin{tcolorbox}[enhanced, colback=white, colframe=primarynavy!40, boxrule=0.8pt, arc=2mm, title={\color{primarynavy}\textbf{Phase I: Requirement Analysis \& System Design (Week 1)}}]
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Analyze statutory Legal Metrology Act provisions and SIH26036 requirements.
        \item Formulate functional specifications and model multi-actor regulatory workflows.
        \item Design the layered Modular Monolith system architecture and PostgreSQL schema.
        \item Establish stateless JWT authentication, password hashing, and Role-Based Access Control (RBAC).
        \item Produce wireframes and modern responsive UI layout designs for all user personas.
    \end{itemize}
\end{tcolorbox}

\begin{tcolorbox}[enhanced, colback=white, colframe=primarynavy!40, boxrule=0.8pt, arc=2mm, title={\color{primarynavy}\textbf{Phase II: Core System Development (Week 2)}}]
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Implement user account management and commercial stakeholder profile modules.
        \item Build the centralized instrument registry with validation for accuracy classes and capacities.
        \item Develop the guided verification and re-verification application submission wizard.
        \item Implement document upload handling for calibration challans, test certificates, and photographs.
        \item Construct the multi-state application status tracking state machine.
    \end{itemize}
\end{tcolorbox}

\begin{tcolorbox}[enhanced, colback=white, colframe=primarynavy!40, boxrule=0.8pt, arc=2mm, title={\color{primarynavy}\textbf{Phase III: Verification, Scheduling \& Certification (Week 3)}}]
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Implement dynamic inspection scheduling and allocation for LMOs and GATC test centres.
        \item Construct the mobile-responsive digital inspection form for recording standard load observations.
        \item Develop automated tolerance checking algorithms against Maximum Permissible Error (MPE) thresholds.
        \item Engineer the ReportLab PDF certificate engine to assemble official government-format certificates.
        \item Integrate dynamic vector QR code synthesis embedding SHA-256 integrity digests.
        \item Deploy the public, unauthenticated certificate verification endpoint.
    \end{itemize}
\end{tcolorbox}

\begin{tcolorbox}[enhanced, colback=white, colframe=primarynavy!40, boxrule=0.8pt, arc=2mm, title={\color{primarynavy}\textbf{Phase IV: Dashboards, Notifications \& System Integration (Week 4)}}]
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Build role-tailored analytics dashboards for Business Owners, Officers, and Administrators.
        \item Implement automated background routines for certificate validity tracking and expiry calculation.
        \item Integrate search, multi-parameter filtering, and audit log exploration capabilities.
        \item Conduct comprehensive functional, integration, and security testing across all subsystems.
        \item Fix defects, finalize user documentation, and deploy the working web prototype.
    \end{itemize}
\end{tcolorbox}
